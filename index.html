About Me

I’m Ahmad Hakim, a Computer Science student at Effat University specializing in building robust, scalable AI systems and full-stack web applications. I’ve developed complex systems including a legal RAG system using Llama models, a full movie review platform in PHP/JS, and a deep-dive research project on Adversarial Machine Learning. In my latest work, I analyzed model robustness against label-flipping data poisoning using the DDXPlus dataset of 1.3 million patient records, achieving a 99.74% weighted F1 score.

My academic work includes serving as a research assistant on Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for LED lifetime prediction. I also bring strong mathematical foundations to my development work, with extensive experience tutoring Discrete Math, Calculus, Linear Algebra, and Statistics.
